The Cause

Giving Back, Together

One of the privileges of being a successful business is having the opportunity to give back. We are deeply committed to uplifting local communities and supporting a wide range of non-profit organizations.

This year, TransPerfect has partnered with Relay For Life, a dynamic movement devoted to supporting cancer patients and their loved ones.

Logistics

Tenran-Tonosu Mountain Trail

Saturday, November 7th

A scenic climb linking Mount Tenran and Mount Tonosu. The path offers switchback forest tracks, natural viewpoints over Hanno, and an accessible ascent suitable for beginners and experienced walkers alike.


Schedule

  • Meeting Time: 10:00 AM
  • Start Time: 10:30 AM
  • Location: Hanno Station
  • Fee: 3,000 JPY (Includes T-shirt, Kids are free)

Note: Surfaces can be slippery after rain. Wear grip shoes and bring water. Transport costs are self-covered.
