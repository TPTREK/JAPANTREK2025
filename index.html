One of the privileges of being a successful business is having the opportunity to give back. We are deeply committed to uplifting local communities and supporting a wide range of non-profit organizations.

The Kamakura Daibutsu Trail

Saturday, November 8, 2025

A beautiful trek full of history and nature. The trail is beginner-friendly, offering a fun challenge for everyone and a chance to connect outside the office.


Schedule

  • Meeting Time: 10:00 AM
  • Start Time: 10:30 AM
  • Location: Koma Bridge (降魔橋) near Kita-Kamakura Station
  • Fee: 3,000 JPY (Includes T-shirt, Kids are free)

Note: Surfaces can be slippery after rain. Wear grip shoes and bring water. Transport costs are self-covered.
